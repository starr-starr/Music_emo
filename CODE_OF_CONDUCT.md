# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in technical discussions.
- Assume good faith and give feedback about work, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information, credentials, or security-sensitive details.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help keep the discussion productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, and knowingly sharing private or illegal material are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository's GitHub issues without including sensitive personal details. The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Security concerns must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the public issue tracker.
